## 1. Home / Hero

Design is solving problems, not decoration — unless decoration solves a problem. Hinside works across brand identity, digital products, print, tools, events, and signage — wherever a problem needs a visual or systemic answer. Every project starts with curiosity and ends with something methodical: built to work, not just to look good.

---

## 2. About / Studio

Hinside's approach was shaped long before it had a name — starting behind the counter of a sign shop, cutting logos and lettering by hand, then sharpening through one of Norway's most selective creative programs and a legendary student-run studio built on ambition over invoicing. From there: full-service branding for some of Norway's strongest names, smaller studios, freelance work, and leading design inside a growing tech company. Different rooms, different stakes, same instinct — treat design as a tool for solving something real.
