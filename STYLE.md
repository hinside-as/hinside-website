# Hinside — Style Guide

## Principle
Design is problem-solving, not decoration — unless decoration solves a problem.
Hinside is curious, experimental, creative, and methodical.

## Color
- Base palette: monochrome, black to white. Deliberately neutral.
- Tonal range must leave enough headroom for subtle hover states (e.g. increasing opacity/transparency on interactive elements).
- No brand accent color on the studio site itself — each portfolio project carries its own accent color, shown without colliding with the neutral host palette.
- Base mode: mostly dark.

## Typography
- Body: JetBrains Mono, weight 300.
- Display/headings: Playfair Display (variable, via Google Fonts/Fontsource). A high-contrast serif, editorial rather than kinetic — contrasts the quiet technical mono body through classical elegance instead of the "weird/expressive" variable-font direction originally scoped (Fraunces, Recursive, Redaction, Yeseva). Final pick.

## Layout & Spacing
- Airy, generous whitespace throughout.
- Not dense or grid-packed — let content breathe.

## Motion
- Subtle motion throughout the site, not just on key moments.
- Motion should feel intentional and restrained, not decorative for its own sake — in line with the core design principle above.

## Imagery
- Mix of image-heavy sections (project photography/renders) and type-driven sections.
- Balance depends on content per page/section.

## Voice/Personality
- Curious, experimental, creative, methodical.
- Confident but not loud — the neutral palette and quiet body type carry restraint; the display font and motion carry the "experimental" edge.
